📋 Ringkasan Database

  • Database: learnhire_db
  • Engine: InnoDB
  • Charset: utf8mb4_general_ci
  • Total Tabel: 6 tabel
  • Target: MariaDB 10.4.32 / MySQL 8.0+
  • Environment: XAMPP

🔑 Primary Keys

  • PK users.id — AUTO_INCREMENT
  • PK companies.id — AUTO_INCREMENT
  • PK job_categories.id — AUTO_INCREMENT
  • PK jobs.id — AUTO_INCREMENT
  • PK company_likes.id — AUTO_INCREMENT BARU
  • PK support_messages.id — AUTO_INCREMENT

🔗 Foreign Keys & Relationship

  • FK jobs.company_id → companies.id (M:1) — ON DELETE RESTRICT
  • FK jobs.category_id → job_categories.id (M:1) — ON DELETE SET NULL
  • FK company_likes.user_id → users.id (M:1) — ON DELETE CASCADE BARU
  • FK company_likes.company_id → companies.id (M:1) — ON DELETE CASCADE BARU
  • FK support_messages.user_id → users.id (M:1) — ON DELETE CASCADE

📐 Cardinality

  • 1 Company → ∞ Jobs (One-to-Many)
  • 1 Job Category → ∞ Jobs (One-to-Many)
  • 1 User → ∞ Company Likes (One-to-Many)
  • 1 Company → ∞ Company Likes (One-to-Many)
  • 1 User → ∞ Support Messages (One-to-Many)
  • User ↔ Company — Many-to-Many via company_likes
